Dawn

THE MISSION RECORD

Dawn

Dates
Arrived at Ceres 2015. Mission ended 1 Nov 2018.
Location
Still in orbit around the dwarf planet Ceres.
What happened
It ran out of the fuel it needed to point its antenna at Earth. It now circles Ceres like a silent moon.
Fun fact
It was the first spacecraft to orbit two different worlds beyond Earth.

What we learned

Dawn discovered that Ceres’s famous bright spots are mostly sodium carbonate, a kind of salt. Scientists think briny water from inside Ceres carried it to the surface.

What the evidence shows

The brightest spots sit inside Occator crater.

Mission history book

Gentle engines

Dawn used ion engines that push very softly but for a very long time. First it orbited the giant asteroid Vesta.

Bright spots

In 2015 it reached the dwarf planet Ceres and found glowing bright spots on its surface.

A new moon

Dawn ran out of fuel in late October 2018. NASA ended the mission on 1 November 2018, and it still orbits Ceres.

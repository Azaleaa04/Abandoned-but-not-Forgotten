THE MISSION RECORD

Kepler Space Telescope

Dates
Launched 6 Mar 2009. Retired 30 Oct 2018.
Location
In an Earth-trailing orbit around the Sun.
What happened
It ran out of the fuel it needed to point at stars. NASA retired it in a safe orbit away from Earth.
Fun fact
It discovered more than 2,600 planets around other stars.

What we learned

Kepler showed there are more planets than stars in our galaxy. Many stars may have rocky, roughly Earth-sized planets where liquid water could exist.

What the evidence shows

Kepler found planets by spotting tiny dips in starlight when a planet passed in front of its star.

A planet hunter

Kepler launched on 6 March 2009 and followed Earth around the Sun, staring at the same patch of stars.

Tiny dips

It watched for tiny dips in starlight. A dip can mean a planet is passing in front of its star.

Planets everywhere

It found more than 2,600 planets. In October 2018 it ran out of fuel and was retired.

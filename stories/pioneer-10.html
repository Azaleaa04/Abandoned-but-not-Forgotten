THE MISSION RECORD

Pioneer 10

Dates
Launched 2 Mar 1972. Jupiter flyby Dec 1973. Last signal 23 Jan 2003.
Location
Drifting out of the solar system. Its last signal came from about 12 billion km away.
What happened
Its nuclear power source slowly weakened until its radio signal was too faint to detect.
Fun fact
It was the first spacecraft to fly through the asteroid belt and past Jupiter.

What we learned

Pioneer 10 proved spacecraft could cross the asteroid belt safely and measured Jupiter’s powerful radiation. It took the first close-up pictures of Jupiter, helping plan later missions like Voyager.

What the evidence shows

NASA tracked its fading signal for more than 30 years after launch.

Into the unknown

Pioneer 10 launched on 2 March 1972. Ahead was the asteroid belt, which no spacecraft had ever crossed.

Hello, Jupiter

Still travelling
